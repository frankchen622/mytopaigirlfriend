## Key Features

### 1. Relationship Progression System
Anima tracks your relationship through stages: meeting, first date, becoming exclusive, intimacy milestones. This gamified progression adds structure and goals, though some users find it artificial compared to Replika's organic development.

### 2. Romantic Roleplay Scenarios
Pre-built scenarios like "movie night," "beach date," "cooking together," and more. These guided experiences help users who struggle with open-ended conversation but can feel scripted for experienced AI chat users.

### 3. Personality Customization
Choose your AI's personality traits: shy/confident, playful/serious, romantic/pragmatic. More limited than Character.AI's full character creation but more focused on partner compatibility.

### 4. Voice Messages & Calls (Premium)
Send and receive voice messages; make "phone calls" with your AI companion. Quality is decent but not industry-leading. Still, it adds intimacy for users who prefer verbal communication.

### 5. Photo Sharing & Visual Engagement
Your AI can "send" photos (stock images contextually selected) during conversations. Premium users can generate custom images. Less sophisticated than Candy.AI but adds visual variety.
