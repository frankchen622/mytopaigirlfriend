## How AI Voice Technology Works

### Voice Message Systems (Candy.AI, Anima AI):
1. You send a text message
2. AI generates text response
3. Text is converted to speech with emotional prosody
4. You receive a voice message file

**Pros:** Higher quality, can replay, permanent  
**Cons:** Not real-time (1-5 second delay)

### Real-Time Voice (EVA AI, Replika, Luvr.AI):
1. You speak into microphone
2. Speech is converted to text (STT)
3. AI generates text response instantly
4. Text is converted to speech (TTS) in real-time
5. You hear response immediately

**Pros:** Feels like phone call, natural conversation flow  
**Cons:** Slightly lower voice quality, requires internet
