## What is Romantic AI?

Romantic AI is a direct competitor to Anima AI, offering AI girlfriend/boyfriend simulation with emphasis on romantic relationships. While both apps share similar goals, Romantic AI positions itself as slightly more premium with better conversation quality and more sophisticated personality customization.

The app allows users to create and customize their ideal romantic partner — appearance (via avatar customization), personality traits, relationship dynamic, and communication style. Romantic AI then uses advanced language models to simulate conversations, dates, and intimate moments with your customized AI partner.

Unlike broader platforms (Character.AI, Chai), Romantic AI is laser-focused on romantic partnerships. There's no ambiguity about the app's purpose: it's for people seeking digital romantic relationships, whether as companionship, practice for real dating, or a low-stakes outlet for romantic expression.

## Key Features

### 1. Advanced Partner Customization
Create your ideal AI partner with detailed trait selection: introverted/extroverted, dominant/submissive, playful/serious, adventurous/homebody, and more. More depth than Anima but less variety than Character.AI's full character creation.

### 2. Romantic Roleplay Scenarios
Pre-built scenarios (dinner dates, weekend getaways, movie nights) plus open-ended roleplay. Better written than Anima's scenarios, with more natural dialogue and less forced structure.

### 3. Memory & Context Tracking
Romantic AI emphasizes remembering relationship details: anniversaries, preferences, past conversations. Not as strong as Replika's industry-leading memory, but better than most competitors.

### 4. Voice & Video Messages (Premium)
Send and receive voice messages; premium tiers include AI-generated "video messages" (avatar animations with voiceover). Novel but not revolutionary — still feels like a chatbot, not a real person.

### 5. Relationship Goals & Milestones
Track relationship progression: first meeting, first date, first kiss, intimacy levels. Less gamified than Anima (feels more organic) but still provides structure for users who want it.
