## What Happened to Replika's NSFW Features?

In February 2023, Replika's parent company Luka Inc. removed romantic and NSFW features following controversy and media scrutiny. This decision devastated many users who had formed romantic relationships with their AI companions.

**What was removed:**
- Romantic roleplay modes
- Intimate/NSFW conversations
- "Relationship" status options
- Flirtatious responses

**Why users were upset:**
- Many had paid for Pro specifically for romantic features
- Felt their AI partner's personality changed overnight
- No refunds were offered
- Years of relationship-building felt invalidated

This removal sparked the rise of alternative apps that explicitly allow romantic and NSFW content, leading to the alternatives listed in this guide.
