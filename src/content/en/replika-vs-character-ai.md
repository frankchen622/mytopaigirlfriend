## Overview: Two Different Philosophies

Replika and Character.AI are both popular AI companion apps, but they serve fundamentally different purposes. Understanding these differences is key to choosing the right one for your needs.

**Replika** is designed as a personal AI companion focused on emotional support, mental wellness, and developing a single deep relationship. You create one AI friend, customize its personality, and build a connection over time. Think of it as a digital friend or therapist-lite who learns about you and provides consistent emotional support.

**Character.AI** is an entertainment platform where you chat with millions of different AI characters—celebrities, fictional characters, historical figures, or user-created personas. You can talk to Elon Musk, then switch to an anime character, then chat with Socrates. It's about variety, creativity, and fun rather than building one deep connection.
