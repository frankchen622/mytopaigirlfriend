## What is Character.AI?

Character.AI is a revolutionary AI chatbot platform that lets you create and interact with AI-powered characters. Unlike traditional AI assistants, Character.AI focuses on personality-driven conversations where each bot has its own distinct traits, speaking style, and knowledge base.

Founded by former Google AI researchers Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas in 2022, the platform has exploded in popularity, especially among younger users. You can chat with pre-made characters ranging from historical figures to fictional characters, or create your own custom AI personas.

The platform uses advanced language models to generate surprisingly coherent and engaging conversations. Characters can remember context from your chats, adapt their responses to your conversation style, and even develop ongoing storylines across multiple sessions.

What sets Character.AI apart is its community-driven approach—millions of user-created characters are available, covering every imaginable personality type, fandom, and use case.
