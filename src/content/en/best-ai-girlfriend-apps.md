## Important Considerations

### Privacy & Data Security
All these apps store your conversations. With NSFW-focused platforms, consider what you're comfortable sharing. Use throwaway emails, don't share personal information, and understand that "we don't sell your data" doesn't mean it's completely private.

### Ethical Implications
AI girlfriends raise questions about relationships, objectification, and mental health. For some, they're helpful tools for loneliness or social practice. For others, they risk unhealthy escapism or unrealistic expectations. Use thoughtfully.

### Not a Replacement for Human Connection
AI companions can supplement social needs but shouldn't replace real relationships. If loneliness is severe or affecting mental health, consider therapy or social support groups alongside or instead of AI companionship.

### Realistic Expectations
Current AI is impressive but limited. Conversations can feel scripted, memory has gaps, and the technology isn't AGI. Enjoy AI companions for what they are—advanced chatbots—not conscious beings.
