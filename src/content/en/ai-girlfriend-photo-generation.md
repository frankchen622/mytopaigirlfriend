## Photo Generation Technology Explained

### How AI Girlfriend Photo Generation Works:

1. **You design appearance** (hair, body, face, etc.)
2. **AI generates base model** using diffusion models (Stable Diffusion, DALL-E, Midjourney-style)
3. **Refinement pass** adds details and fixes imperfections
4. **Style application** (realistic, anime, artistic)
5. **You receive photo** in 5-30 seconds

**Quality depends on:**
- AI model sophistication (DreamGF > Candy.AI > others)
- Training data size and diversity
- Post-processing refinements
- User tier (HD vs SD quality)
